ABOUT THE APP

A less noisy
morning.

Alarm Clock is being shaped as a simple and clear alarm experience for quieter mornings, better rhythm, and a more personal start to the day.

01 // CLEAR

One clear moment

The direction is intentionally focused: make the important information easy to see when you are barely awake.

02 // CALM

A gentler rhythm

The aim is to make waking up feel less cluttered and more considered, without turning your first minutes into a task list.

03 // YOURS

Made to fit you

The experience is designed around the idea that there is no single perfect morning — only the one that works for you.
