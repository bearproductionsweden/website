GUIDE

How to
play.

The rules are simple. The fun is in reading the board, leaving the right choices for later, and opening space for the next match.

BOARD // OPEN TILES

萬中●發中●萬發
Tray: match pairs to clear

Find open tiles

Choose tiles that are not covered by another tile and have at least one long side free. These are the tiles you can move.

Match a pair

Tap two matching open tiles to place them in your tray. A pair disappears and opens up more possibilities.

Build combos

Match quickly, one after another, to build combos and raise your score. In Relaxed mode, you can also take it as slow as you like.

Use help when you need it

Hint shows a possible pair, Undo takes back a choice, and Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles in a fair new order.

FAQ

Common
questions.

Here are answers to the most common questions about Red Lotus and how the game works.

What is the goal of a level?

Clear the board by matching pairs of identical open tiles. When every tile is gone, the level is complete.

Can a level become impossible?

No. Every level has a guaranteed solution. Some choices can make the next step harder, so use Hint, Undo, or Shuffle if you get stuck.

What is the difference between Relaxed and Timed?

Relaxed has no time limit and is made for calmer play. Timed adds a countdown and bonus gold when you finish in time.

What do Hint, Undo, and Shuffle do?

Hint points out a possible pair. Undo reverses your latest move. Shuffle rearranges the remaining tiles into a new fair order.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your completed levels, stars, gold, and settings are stored locally on your own device.

Does the game work without internet?

Yes. Red Lotus is fully playable offline and does not use the internet while you play.
