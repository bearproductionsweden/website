A plain-language overview of how Mahjong Solitaire: Red Lotus handles data.

Mahjong Solitaire: Red Lotus
Bear Production — Last updated: July 10, 2026

The short version

This game does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data collection

Mahjong Solitaire: Red Lotus collects no data whatsoever. The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests during play.

Game progress

Your game progress (completed levels, star ratings, gold, and settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and it is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. The app is rated 4+ and contains no ads, purchases, links, or social features.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles data (for example, adding online leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the app's update notes.
