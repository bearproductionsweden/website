ABOUT THE GAME

The signal
is fading.

Last Signal is built around fast decisions under pressure: survive long enough to grow stronger, then decide whether to push onward or find a way out.

01 // HOLD THE LINE

Waves close in

Stay mobile as enemy waves build around you. Every moment matters when the arena turns hostile.

02 // ADAPT

Build the right arsenal

Shape a run with upgrades and tools that change how you fight. The best choice depends on the sector in front of you.

03 // GET OUT

Reach extraction

Push through dangerous sectors, face tougher threats, and aim for the next extraction point before the signal is lost.

FAQ

Common
questions.

Last Signal is in development. These answers reflect the direction of the project today and will be updated as the game grows.

What kind of game is Last Signal?

It is a dark, post-apocalyptic survivor roguelike with fast runs, waves of enemies, upgrades, bosses, and extraction-focused progression.

When will it be released?

There is no release date to share yet. The focus right now is making the core game feel sharp, readable, and worth replaying.

Which platforms will it support?

Platforms have not been announced yet. They will be shared here when that part of the plan is ready.

Will every run be the same?

No. The current direction includes changing sectors, different upgrade choices, and runs that ask you to adapt to the threats in front of you.

Will there be a Privacy Policy?

Yes. A complete Privacy Policy will be published before any public release, once the game's final data practices are confirmed.

Where can I follow development?

Updates will appear on Bearproductions channels as the project reaches milestones. More ways to follow will be added here when they are ready.
